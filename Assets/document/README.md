Functional Requirements
-Member Registration: Capture full name, age (16+), phone number, ID proof, and emergency contact. Assign a unique Member ID.
-Membership Plan Rules: Support different durations (monthly, quarterly, yearly), fee structures, and single active plan constraints per user.
-Billing and Payments: Handle recurring automated billing, manual cash or card logs, and renewal alerts.
-Check-In and Attendance: Track daily visits via mobile app, card, or biometric scan.
-Admin and Reporting: Generate revenue summaries, active member counts, and expiration lists.

Non-Functional Requirements
-Security: Encrypt member personal data and secure payment processing gateways.
-Performance: Ensure fast check-in and transaction processing under 2 seconds, even during peak operational hours.
-Usability: Provide a simple, clear interface for both gym staff and members using self-service portals.
